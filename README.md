**AP World History: Modern.** 954 AP-style questions across all nine units, with an explanation for every answer choice. It has 45 mock quizzes (5 per unit), topic drills, a mixed mock weighted like the exam, practice and timed exam modes, and saved progress.

**AP Cybersecurity.** 666 AP-style questions across all five units and 24 topics, with an explanation for every answer choice. 66 of them are source-based sets built on logs, rule tables and policies. It also has:
- 25 mock quizzes, topic drills, and a mixed mock (60 questions in 80 minutes)
- practice and timed exam modes
- 24 topic lessons
- 12 written-response practice questions with scoring guides
- 11 hands-on labs: phishing triage, risk matrix, floor plan, badge logs, network zoning, firewall ACLs, SIEM hunt, hashing, auth logs, chmod, ciphers

[AP_Cybersecurity_Tap_to_Check.pdf](cyber/AP_Cybersecurity_Tap_to_Check.pdf) is a printable or tablet version: tap an answer to jump to its explanation.

**SAT Daily.** Two questions from each of the eight SAT skill areas every day: 16 questions, about 30 minutes. Every answer choice has an explanation. It has 901 Reading and Writing questions, about four months of new ones, and the math is generated fresh every day. Missed questions come back as retries. It tracks a streak calendar and accuracy by area. Back up progress and Restore move progress between devices. Source and build scripts are in [sat/src](sat/src).

**Rational Expressions Prep.** Algebra 2 lessons 4.3–4.5: simplifying and domain, multiplying and dividing, adding and subtracting, complex fractions, rational equations and extraneous solutions, systems, and work problems. It has:
- 8 lessons with step-through worked examples and a Listen button
- 131 practice questions, each with a worked solution and an explanation for every wrong choice
- speed drills with fresh questions every round (90-second sprint, 10-question time trial)
- a 15-question, 25-minute test sim
- all 12 problems from the 4.3–4.5 review sheet, solved step by step

**Topic 4 Prep.** The full Algebra 2 Topic 4 test review: inverse variation, the reciprocal function and its transformations, key features and graphs of rational functions (holes, asymptotes, intercepts, end behavior), plus everything in Rational Expressions Prep. It is a separate app with its own saved progress, so progress in Rational Expressions Prep is kept. It has:
- 11 lessons with step-through worked examples and a Listen button
- 231 practice questions (54 easy, 110 medium, 67 hard), each with a worked solution and an explanation for every wrong choice; graph questions are drawn on a grid
- every problem from the Topic 4 test review, as practice questions and solved step by step, alongside the 4.3–4.5 review sheet
- speed drills with a Functions focus (asymptotes, holes, inverse variation, horizontal asymptotes)
- a 20-question, 30-minute test sim weighted like the Topic 4 review

**Rational Expressions, Detailed.** A version of Rational Expressions Prep built around typed answers. It keeps its own progress, so it can sit beside the other Algebra apps. It has:
- answers typed on a math keypad and checked for equivalence, with common mistakes named (a lost restriction, an extraneous root, a sign slip); choices are one tap away
- fresh numbers on every question from 35 templates, plus the 131 original questions
- hints in three steps (a nudge, the first step, the full solution) and My Mistakes, where every miss comes back with new numbers
- mastery (3 right in a row without hints) with reviews on days 1, 3, 7 and 21
- a daily 5-minute test, lesson quizzes, a 25-question unit test, the test sim, a word-problem workshop and a boss challenge
- voice lessons that highlight each step as they're read, with "Explain it another way" and We-do checks
- three speed formats with medals, and a Daily Dash
- XP, levels, coins, daily quests, badges, streaks and rest days
- a PIN-locked parent page, plus Back up progress and Restore

Source and build scripts are in [alg2-detailed/src](alg2-detailed/src).

**Chemistry.** Chemistry SC300A/B, all 10 units, in the same style as Rational Expressions, Detailed. It keeps its own progress. It has:
- 38 voice lessons in course order that highlight each step as they're read, with worked examples, traps, "Explain it another way" and two We-do checks
- typed answers: a keypad for numbers (with scientific notation and sig figs), boxes for balancing coefficients, and the keyboard for formulas, names and electron configurations; common mistakes are named, and choices are one tap away
- fresh numbers on every question from 124 templates, plus 61 concept questions that explain every wrong choice
- hints in three steps and My Mistakes, where every miss comes back with new numbers
- mastery (3 right in a row without hints) with reviews on days 1, 3, 7 and 21
- a daily 5-minute test, lesson quizzes, 10 unit tests, SC300A and SC300B finals, a course final, a concept test, a problem workshop and a boss challenge
- a reference with a tap-for-details periodic table, common ions, an equation sheet and constants, also one tap away from any question
- 20 speed drills in three formats with medals, and a Daily Dash
- XP, levels, coins, daily quests, badges, streaks and rest days
- a PIN-locked parent page, plus Back up progress and Restore

Source and build scripts are in [chem/src](chem/src).

**English 10H.** Honors English 10 at Issaquah High (Honors Lit Analysis & Composition 2), following Ira's 2026–27 syllabus: ten units, five a semester, with Socratic seminars all year. Units 1 (personal narrative), 3, 4 and 5 are ready, plus the writing tools for every unit. It has:
- Unit 3, Fahrenheit 451: 7 section guides across the novel's three parts, with summaries, key events, a craft focus, quote hunts and seminar questions, hidden past the section Ira has read
- Unit 4, A Small Place: 4 section guides with Kincaid's rhetorical moves, voices, motifs, a model SOAPSTone and a SOAPSTone tool
- Unit 5, the argument workshop: 8 lessons, a 14-fallacy gallery, public-domain speeches to analyze, practice sources for judging credibility, and debate prep
- 88 reading checks, 55 argument questions, a fallacy finder, a terms quiz from a 95-term glossary, timed tests for Units 3, 4 and 5, and My misses
- character, symbol and theme trackers, and the syllabus's course goals
- assignments planned day by day, a prompt decoder, thesis and outline builders, a Quote Log with MLA citations, seminar prep
- 5 essay guides with rubrics and model paragraphs, 28 writing drills, and 14 timed writes
- an offline draft checker that marks thesis, evidence, citation, commentary and style problems in the draft, rubric self-scoring, and a feedback-only hand-off to Claude with an AI-use log
- Coach: what to do next and why; review cards, daily goals, streaks, badges, backup and a parent summary

Source and build scripts are in [english10/src](english10/src).

**Driver Workbench.** Weekly GSPro range exports for two golfers. The Windows PC beside GSPro is the import station: each golfer's CSV files are dropped there, sorted by club speed and saved with their original lines. The iPad and the Mac show the analysis by player: driver trends, dispersion, strike checks, a realistic outlook and whole-bag gapping. Unlike the apps above, it runs as an installed Claude app, because its data lives in a private Claude database that both devices share; nothing is stored on this site. Code, tests and install steps are in [driver-workbench](driver-workbench/README.md).

## Put an app on an iPad

1. Open the app's link in Safari.
2. Tap Share (or ••• and then Share), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open it once from the new icon while online. After that it works offline.

Each app gets its own icon. Progress is saved on each device, inside the app. Deleting the icon deletes that device's progress.

If the Cyber, SAT, Rational Expressions, Topic 4, Detailed or English 10H link shows World History the first time on an iPad that already has World History installed, wait a few seconds and reload the page once.

## Updating

Changing any file here republishes the site. Installed copies pick up the new version the next time they open online.

The Driver Workbench is the exception: it is republished to Claude from [driver-workbench/app](driver-workbench/app), as its README describes.
